# My Personal AI Usage Rules

## Rule 1 - What I will never paste

I will never paste credentials, environment values, personal data, or unidentified real external data into an AI tool. I will also remove local paths, private URLs, registry details, account details, and production configuration. When the real values are not needed, I will use made-up data or clear placeholders instead.

## Rule 2 - What I will always verify before accepting

I will accept AI-generated work only when I can explain it line by line and have checked it in the right way. I will run focused tests for backend code, check frontend behavior in the browser, review every important CI and Docker setting, and grade security findings against evidence from the repository.

## Rule 3 - How I will record AI contributions

I will add each AI contribution to my governance worksheet. I will record what the AI generated, the course module, whether I understand it line by line, and what I did to verify it. For security findings, I will also record my final decision: Valid, False Positive, or Noise.
